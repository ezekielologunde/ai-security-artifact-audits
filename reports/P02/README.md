# Preliminary model-provenance consistency study

Ezekiel Ologunde. Seven-page ACM-style technical preprint. First released
20 October 2026; literature-only revision 22 November 2026 adds the direct
ReleaseSeal and knowyourai comparison and qualifies the candidate contribution.
The abstract, research questions, methods, results, tables, discussion and conclusion
remain unchanged. No experiment was rerun for this publication revision.

The public supplement reproduces only the original 36 developer-authored controlled
cases, five implementation variants and seven tests. All 14 reproduction files
retain their previous bytes. See reproduction/README.md. The frozen reproduction
README records its historical preparation context; this page and RIGHTS.md provide
current publication and authored licensing information.

The public supplement excludes natural cards, metadata snapshots, downloaded weights,
third-party comparator source and reviewer material. It does not independently
reproduce the natural 59-repository cohort or establish natural-case accuracy.
The original private archive was separately replayed, as described in the paper.

The broad idea of checking documentation against artifacts already overlaps direct
comparators. No field-wide priority, generic new detector, improved malware detection
or matched comparative efficacy is established. The narrower claim/evidence endpoint
remains a research task. External peer review is not required for preprint release.
